COAA Connect: Why Good Projects Have Project Charters


Interactive: What is the biggest key to facilitating open dialog?

Audience Responses:

  • Can you provide more specific examples of how these methods actually worked in practice, rather than just theoretical applications?
  • I'm studying project management and I'd love to know more about how this feedback-gathering process could be adapted for a team-based setting.
  • How do you measure the effectiveness of gathering stakeholder feedback? What metrics or KPIs would you use?
  • What was the sample size and demographics of your stakeholder group? Were there any notable outliers or non-responders?
  • I'm new to this field, can you explain what 'stakeholders' are and how they fit into this process in simple terms?
  • How do you prioritize which stakeholders to gather feedback from first? Was there a clear methodology or criteria used?
  • I noticed that some of your methods seemed quite resource-intensive (e.g., surveys). What strategies did you use to minimize environmental impact?
  • What tools or software did you use to facilitate the feedback-gathering process? Are there any recommended solutions for stakeholders who aren't tech-savvy?
  • How do you ensure that stakeholder feedback is accurately translated into actionable insights, rather than just collecting noise or opinions?
  • Can you discuss the theoretical underpinnings of your approach? How does it align with existing research in the field of stakeholder engagement?
  • What strategies did you use to encourage stakeholders to provide more candid or honest feedback, rather than just providing generic responses?
  • How do you handle conflicting opinions or diverse perspectives within a stakeholder group? What tactics do you use to maintain constructive dialogue?
  • Can you walk us through how you integrate stakeholder feedback into the decision-making process? How much weight does feedback carry in your organization?
  • How did gathering stakeholder feedback impact organizational culture and dynamics within your team or organization?
  • What are some potential pitfalls or challenges that stakeholders may face when providing feedback? How do you mitigate these risks?

Charter-style Synthesis:

  • The importance of practical application and real-world examples was a recurring theme, with many respondents seeking more concrete details on how methods worked in practice.
  • There was a need for clarity and definition around the concept of "stakeholders," with some respondents seeking simplified explanations or examples.
  • Metrics and KPIs were identified as essential for measuring effectiveness, with respondents seeking guidance on how to track and prioritize stakeholder feedback.
