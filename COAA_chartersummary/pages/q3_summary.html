COAA Connect: Why Good Projects Have Project Charters

Interactive: What aspect of the project charter process would you like to know more about before implementing on one of your projects?

Audience Responses:

  • Onboarding additional team members with all the great context from the Pre Design Phase
  • Engagement of stakeholders
  • How to create the schedule after the post-it portion.
  • Where to post final product
  • What’s the final product? How is it used?
  • How does it set up accountability? This is typically part of a charter process.
  • Was it a deliverable in the RFP?
  • Identifying the right people in the room
  • Buy-in
  • Keeping people engaged
  • Any pitfalls
  • Disagreement among stakeholders
  • Who develops and owns? Owner? Architect?
  • Methodologies for resolving conflicting aspirations
  • Can this be done ahead of the construction phase?
  • Initial category selection
  • Delivery of the charter
  • Establishing the principles prior to the charter process
  • Does this process involve the contractor too?
  • How does one get buy in from leaders to initiate a charter?
  • Data processing
  • How do you resolve completely different goals or approaches?
  • How to align desires with budget so as not to give participants unrealistic expectations.
  • How do you ensure participation?
  • How early should a charter be started?
  • As the Team grows to include more stakeholders, specifically the Build Team, how does the Charter evolve to accommodate new priorities focused on execution and delivery of the project.
  • Is this solely the responsibility of client and its end users to define.
  • How scalable is the process? What would it look like on smaller projects?
  • Defining program mission
  • Addressing conflicting project goals amongst stakeholders that arise during charter development
  • What is the end deliverable, a chart? Do you start future meeting with a reminder of the charter

Charter-style Synthesis:

  • Key theme: Understanding the purpose and scope of the final product and its intended use.
  • Common concern: Ensuring buy-in from leaders and stakeholders to initiate and move forward with the project charter process.
  • Major consideration: Identifying and addressing conflicting goals, priorities, and aspirations among stakeholders during the charter development phase.
